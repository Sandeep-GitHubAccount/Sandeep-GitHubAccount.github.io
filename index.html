Hello, I am
Dr. Sandeep Khanna

I am currently serving as an Assistant Professor at FLAME University. My research interests lie in the area of Food Computing, with a focus on Food Analysis, Recommendation Systems, and Food Recognition. My work leverages advanced methodologies in Social Network Analysis, Semi-supervised Learning, Computer Vision, and Deep Learning to develop intelligent and data-driven solutions for real-world applications. I am particularly interested in designing scalable and interpretable models that bridge the gap between computational techniques and practical deployment in food and health-related domains.


More About Me

In addition to my primary research in Food Computing, I have also been actively working in the domains of diagrammatic reasoning, 2D-to-3D character conversion, document understanding, and object detection.
